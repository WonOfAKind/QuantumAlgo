<p style="font: 10pt Times New Roman, Times, Serif; margin: 0pt 0; text-align: justify"><i>(c) Appointment of Principal Accounting Officer</i></p>

<p style="font: 10pt Times New Roman, Times, Serif; margin: 0pt 0; text-align: justify">&#160;</p>

<p style="font: 10pt Times New Roman, Times, Serif; margin: 0pt 0; text-align: justify; text-indent: 0.5in">On July 26, 2022, Roblox Corporation
(the &#8220;Company&#8221;) appointed Amy Rawlings to the position of Chief Accounting Officer, effective July 26, 2022. In this position,
she will serve as the Company&#8217;s Principal Accounting Officer for the purposes of the Securities Exchange Act of 1934, as amended.
Deepika Gupta will cease serving in the capacity of interim Principal Accounting Officer of the Company upon the effective date of Ms.
Rawlings&#8217; appointment and will continue to serve as the Company&#8217;s Senior Director of Technical Accounting and Reporting.</p>

<p style="font: 10pt Times New Roman, Times, Serif; margin: 0pt 0; text-align: justify; text-indent: 0.5in">&#160;</p>

<p style="font: 10pt Times New Roman, Times, Serif; margin: 0pt 0; text-align: justify; text-indent: 0.5in">Ms. Rawlings, 38, previously
served as the Chief Accounting Officer of Zynga Inc. from August 2021 to July 2022. Between June 2010 and August 2021, Ms. Rawlings served
in various roles at Zynga Inc., most recently as its Vice President &#8211; Corporate Controller. From October 2006 to May 2010, Ms. Rawlings
served as an Audit Senior at Ernst &amp; Young LLP. Ms. Rawlings received a B.A. in Business Economics with an emphasis in Accounting
from the University of California, Santa Barbara, and is a Certified Public Accountant in the state of California.</p>

<p style="font: 10pt Times New Roman, Times, Serif; margin: 0pt 0; text-align: justify; text-indent: 0.5in">&#160;</p>

<p style="font: 10pt Times New Roman, Times, Serif; margin: 0pt 0; text-align: justify; text-indent: 0.5in">In connection with Ms. Rawlings&#8217;
appointment as Chief Accounting Officer, she will receive an annual base salary of $500,000 and restricted stock units covering shares
of the Company&#8217;s Class A common stock valued at $3.0&#160;million, determined by dividing that amount by the 20 trading day average
closing price of the Company&#8217;s Class A Common Stock on the New York Stock Exchange (the &#8220;RSU Award&#8221;). The RSU Award
will vest subject to Ms. Rawlings&#8217; continued employment with the Company on the applicable vesting dates, as may be determined by
the Company&#8217;s Board of Directors. The RSU Award will be subject to the Company&#8217;s 2020 Equity Incentive Plan and Form of Restricted
Stock Unit Agreement. She will additionally be eligible for quarterly equity refresh awards, in accordance with the Company&#8217;s current
practices. The Company will also enter into its standard form of indemnification agreement with Ms. Rawlings.</p>
