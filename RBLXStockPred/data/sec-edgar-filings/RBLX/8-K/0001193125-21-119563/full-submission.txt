 <P STYLE="margin-top:0pt; margin-bottom:0pt; font-size:10pt; font-family:Times New Roman"><B>Item&nbsp;5.02 Departure of Directors or Certain Officers; Election of Directors; Appointment of Certain
Officers; Compensatory Arrangements of Certain Officers. </B></P> <P STYLE="margin-top:6pt; margin-bottom:0pt; font-size:10pt; font-family:Times New Roman"><I>(d) Election of Director </I></P>
<P STYLE="margin-top:12pt; margin-bottom:0pt; text-indent:4%; font-size:10pt; font-family:Times New Roman">On April&nbsp;15, 2021, the Board of Directors (the &#147;Board&#148;) of Roblox Corporation (the &#147;Company&#148;) elected Gina
Mastantuono, age 50, to serve on the Board, effective immediately. Ms.&nbsp;Mastantuono will serve as a Class&nbsp;I director, with a term expiring at the Company&#146;s annual meeting of stockholders to be held in 2022. In addition,
Ms.&nbsp;Mastantuono was appointed to serve as the Chair of the Audit Committee of the Board (the &#147;Audit Committee&#148;). </P> <P STYLE="margin-top:12pt; margin-bottom:0pt; text-indent:4%; font-size:10pt; font-family:Times New Roman">Gina
Mastantuono has served as the Chief Financial Officer of ServiceNow, Inc. since January 2020. Prior to ServiceNow, she was CFO of Ingram Micro, Inc., a leading provider of global technology and supply chain services. She also held various executive
finance roles at Revlon, Inc., where she was the SVP, chief accounting officer and international CFO, InterActiveCorp, and Triarc Companies Inc. Ms.&nbsp;Mastantuono holds a B.S. degree in Accounting and Business Administration from the State
University of New York at Albany. </P> <P STYLE="margin-top:12pt; margin-bottom:0pt; text-indent:4%; font-size:10pt; font-family:Times New Roman">In connection with her election to our Board on April&nbsp;15, 2021, and in accordance with the
Company&#146;s Outside Director Compensation Policy (the &#147;Policy&#148;), a copy of which is filed as Exhibit 10.9 to Amendment No.&nbsp;2 to the Company&#146;s Registration Statement on Form <FONT STYLE="white-space:nowrap">S-1</FONT> and is
incorporated herein in its entirety by reference, Ms.&nbsp;Mastantuono is eligible to participate in the Company&#146;s standard compensation arrangements for Outside Directors (as defined in the Policy). Under the terms of such arrangements,
Ms.&nbsp;Mastantuono will receive an annual cash retainer of $180,000 for her service on the Board, an annual cash retainer of $25,000 for her service on as Chair of the Audit Committee and certain equity grants as specified in the Policy. Pursuant
to the Policy, Ms.&nbsp;Mastantuono automatically received (i)&nbsp;an initial equity award of restricted stock units having an approximate value equal to $360,000, which is scheduled to vest over three years, and (ii)&nbsp;an annual equity award of
restricted stock units having an approximate value equal to $180,000. The number of shares subject to such awards is determined by dividing the value of the applicable award by the average fair market value of a share of our Class&nbsp;A common
stock for that number of consecutive trading days starting on March&nbsp;10, 2021 and ending on the trading day prior to the grant date. </P>
<P STYLE="margin-top:12pt; margin-bottom:0pt; text-indent:4%; font-size:10pt; font-family:Times New Roman">The Company will enter into its standard form of indemnification agreement with Ms.&nbsp;Mastantuono, the form of which has been filed as
Exhibit&nbsp;10.1 to the Company&#146;s Registration Statement on Form <FONT STYLE="white-space:nowrap">S-1</FONT> and is incorporated herein in its entirety by reference. </P>
<P STYLE="margin-top:12pt; margin-bottom:0pt; text-indent:4%; font-size:10pt; font-family:Times New Roman">There is no arrangement or understanding between Ms.&nbsp;Mastantuono and any other persons pursuant to which Ms.&nbsp;Mastantuono was elected
as a director of the Company. Other than the indemnification agreement described in the foregoing paragraph, Ms.&nbsp;Mastantuono is not a party to any transaction, or series of transactions, required to be disclosed pursuant to Item 404(a) of
Regulation <FONT STYLE="white-space:nowrap">S-K.</FONT> </P> <P STYLE="margin-top:12pt; margin-bottom:0pt; text-indent:4%; font-size:10pt; font-family:Times New Roman">On April&nbsp;15, 2021, the Company issued a press release announcing
Ms.&nbsp;Mastantuono&#146;s appointment to the Board. The press release is attached hereto as Exhibit 99.1 and incorporated herein in its entirety by reference. </P> <P STYLE="font-size:18pt;margin-top:0pt;margin-bottom:0pt">&nbsp;</P>

<P STYLE="margin-top:12pt; margin-bottom:0pt; font-size:10pt; font-family:Times New Roman" ALIGN="center"><B>Gina Mastantuono Joins Roblox&#146;s Board of Directors </B></P>
<P STYLE="margin-top:12pt; margin-bottom:0pt; font-size:10pt; font-family:Times New Roman">April&nbsp;15, 2021 </P> <P STYLE="margin-top:12pt; margin-bottom:0pt; font-size:10pt; font-family:Times New Roman">SAN MATEO, Calif.&#151;(BUSINESS
WIRE)&#151;Roblox (NYSE: RBLX), a global platform bringing millions of people together through shared experiences, today announced the appointment of Gina Mastantuono to the company&#146;s board of directors. Mastantuono will also serve as
Chairperson for Roblox&#146;s Audit Committee. Mastantuono is a highly-skilled financial executive who has led financial and strategic business initiatives for more than 25 years for innovative global brands, including her current role as Chief
Financial Officer of ServiceNow, Inc., a global enterprise software company that delivers digital workflows. Mastantuono&#146;s appointment was effective April&nbsp;15, 2021. </P>
<P STYLE="margin-top:12pt; margin-bottom:0pt; font-size:10pt; font-family:Times New Roman">&#147;Gina Mastantuono is an eminent finance authority, and her global experience is a great enhancement to the Board&#146;s perspective,&#148; said David
Baszucki, CEO of Roblox. &#147;As a public company, having Gina&#146;s technical financial expertise, as well as her sharp strategic skills and integrity related to risk oversight will be invaluable as she chairs the Audit Committee.&#148; </P>
<P STYLE="margin-top:12pt; margin-bottom:0pt; font-size:10pt; font-family:Times New Roman">&#147;I am honored to join Roblox, a pioneer in enabling shared experiences online and in virtual worlds. Roblox is building the metaverse, enabling people
around the world to play, work, learn, and socialize together in a way that simulates the real world,&#148; said Gina Mastantuono. &#147;I am excited to join Roblox&#146;s board in helping to bring this vision to life.&#148; </P>
<P STYLE="margin-top:12pt; margin-bottom:0pt; font-size:10pt; font-family:Times New Roman">As Chief Financial Officer of ServiceNow, Inc. Gina develops and leads their world class team focused on driving customer success, spanning across all aspects
of finance. Prior to ServiceNow, she was CFO of Ingram Micro, Inc., a leading provider of global technology and supply chain services. She also held various executive finance roles at Revlon, Inc., where she was the SVP, chief accounting officer,
and international CFO, InterActiveCorp, and Triarc Companies Inc. </P> <P STYLE="margin-top:12pt; margin-bottom:0pt; font-size:10pt; font-family:Times New Roman">Mastantuono has also been honored as one of CRN&#146;s &#147;Power 100&#148; and twice
listed on the National Diversity Council&#146;s Top 50 Most Powerful Women in Technology. </P> <P STYLE="margin-top:12pt; margin-bottom:0pt; font-size:10pt; font-family:Times New Roman">Mastantuono joins board chairman David Baszucki, lead
independent director Anthony Lee, and existing board members Greg Baszucki, Chris Carvalho, and Andrea Wong. </P> <P STYLE="margin-top:18pt; margin-bottom:0pt; font-size:10pt; font-family:Times New Roman"><B>About Roblox </B></P>
<P STYLE="margin-top:6pt; margin-bottom:0pt; font-size:10pt; font-family:Times New Roman">Roblox&#146;s mission is to build a human <FONT STYLE="white-space:nowrap">co-experience</FONT> platform that enables shared experiences among billions of
users. Every day, more than 32&nbsp;million people around the world have fun with friends as they explore millions of immersive digital experiences. All of these experiences are built by the Roblox community, made up of over eight million creators.
We believe in building a safe, civil, and diverse community&#151;one that inspires and fosters creativity and positive relationships between people around the world. For more information, please visit corp.roblox.com. </P>
