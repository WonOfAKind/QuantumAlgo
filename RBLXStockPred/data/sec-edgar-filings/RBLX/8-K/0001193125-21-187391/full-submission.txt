<P STYLE="margin-top:6pt; margin-bottom:0pt; text-indent:4%; font-size:10pt; font-family:Times New Roman">On June&nbsp;10, 2021, Roblox Corporation (the &#147;Company&#148;) issued a press release announcing that, on June&nbsp;9, 2021, a number of

Court for the Central District of California, captioned ABKCO Music, Inc.; Big Machine Music, LLC; Boosey&nbsp;&amp; Hawkes, Inc.; Cherio Corporation; Concord Music Publishing LLC; Concord Music Group, Inc.; Downtown Music Publishing LLC; Hipgnosis

Universal Music &#150; Z Tunes LLC; Universal Music Corp.; Universal Music Publishing, Inc.; Universal Musica, Inc., Plaintiffs v. Roblox Corporation, Defendant, alleging that the Company engaged in copyright infringement by using certain musical
compositions on the Roblox platform without necessary licenses.&nbsp;The Company intends to vigorously defend itself against the claims set forth in the complaint, and given the recent filing, the Company cannot predict the ultimate outcome of such
litigation and has not come to a view on the value of any ultimate outcome at this time.</P> <P STYLE="margin-top:12pt; margin-bottom:0pt; text-indent:4%; font-size:10pt; font-family:Times New Roman">A copy of the press release is attached hereto as

<P STYLE="margin-top:12pt; margin-bottom:0pt; font-size:10pt; font-family:Times New Roman"><B>Addressing Recently Filed Litigation by Certain Music Publishers </B></P>
<P STYLE="margin-top:6pt; margin-bottom:0pt; font-size:10pt; font-family:Times New Roman">As a platform powered by a community of creators, we are passionate about protecting intellectual property rights &#150; from independent artists and
songwriters, to music labels and publishers &#150; and require all Roblox community members to abide by our Community Rules. We do not tolerate copyright infringement, which is why we use industry-leading, advanced filtering technology to detect and
prohibit unauthorized recordings. We expeditiously respond to any valid Digital Millennium Copyright Act (DMCA) request by removing any infringing content and, in accordance with our stringent repeat infringer policy, taking action against anyone
violating our rules. </P> <P STYLE="margin-top:12pt; margin-bottom:0pt; font-size:10pt; font-family:Times New Roman">We are surprised and disappointed by this lawsuit which represents a fundamental misunderstanding of how the Roblox platform
operates, and will defend Roblox vigorously as we work to achieve a fair resolution.</P> <P STYLE="margin-top:12pt; margin-bottom:0pt; font-size:10pt; font-family:Times New Roman">We believe the Roblox metaverse provides a massive opportunity for
the music industry, and have partnered with major labels and publishers to host successful music events, attended by millions of fans,&nbsp;for such artists as Ava Max, Lil Nas X, Why Don&#146;t We, Royal Blood, and Zara Larsson. We are committed to
continuing to partner with the music industry to unlock new, creative, and commercial opportunities for artists and songwriters through virtual merchandise, exclusive virtual concerts, Launch Parties, and more. </P>
